About

Landscapes, wildflowers, and the odd Cambodian temple.

My work starts as a loose painting in Indian ink or watercolour, usually inspired by wherever I've just been — the Long Mynd on a wet Tuesday, or the streets of Barcelona. I build up texture in mixed media, then finish with free machine embroidery, so no two pieces come out quite the same.

These days most of it starts closer to home, in the Shropshire hills or my own overgrown garden.
