Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you visit delny.org or get in touch through it. It's written in plain language — if anything's unclear, use the contact details below.

What this site collects

This site does not use analytics, tracking cookies, or advertising scripts of any kind. It does not require an account or login. The only personal information collected is what you choose to submit through the contact form:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Whatever you write in the message

This is used only to reply to your enquiry — about commissions, tuition, workshops, or anything else you've asked about. It isn't used for marketing, added to a mailing list, or shared or sold to anyone else.

Third-party services this site uses

A few outside services are involved in running this site, each only for the specific job below:

  • Formspree — processes contact form submissions and delivers them by email. See Formspree's privacy policy.
  • PayPal / Stripe — if you buy something through the shop, payment is handled entirely on PayPal's or Stripe's own site. This site never sees or stores your card or payment details.
  • Google Drive / Google Sheets — used behind the scenes to host artwork photos and page content (gallery, events, shop listings, news posts). These don't collect any information about site visitors.

Cookies

This site doesn't set any cookies of its own. If that changes in future, this policy will be updated to say so.

How long information is kept

Messages sent through the contact form are kept only as long as needed to respond to you and for a reasonable period afterwards, then deleted.

Your rights

If you're in the UK or EU, you have rights under data protection law (UK GDPR / GDPR) to ask what information is held about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted. To exercise any of these, get in touch using the details below.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: get in touch via the contact form.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated occasionally to reflect changes to the site. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
